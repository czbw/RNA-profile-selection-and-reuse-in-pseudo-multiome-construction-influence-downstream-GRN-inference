Supplementary Figure Sx. Agreement with the native network across RNA reuse levels.

BMMC networks inferred under inferred and random pairing were compared with the SCENIC+ network inferred from native same-cell RNA and ATAC measurements at reuse ratios of 1.000, 1.406, 2.008. The eligible RNA population, ATAC input and within-donor-cell-type multiplicity distributions were matched between pairing rules at each reuse level. (a) Spearman correlation of region-gene correlation coefficients across candidate pairs. (b) Jaccard index of the top 100,000 region-gene pairs ranked by importance. (c) Jaccard index of the top 100,000 TF-gene edges ranked by importance. (d,e) Median target-gene and target-region Jaccard indices across shared eRegulons. (f,g) Jaccard indices of signed eRegulon identities and TF identities. Red triangles indicate inferred pairing; blue circles indicate random pairing within strata. Marker fills become lighter at higher reuse, following the main Figure 3 palette. The grey band marks the reuse range of the corrected pAML construction (1.3625 at diagnosis and 1.4302 at relapse). Black horizontal lines indicate perfect agreement. Panel a uses an expanded vertical scale from 0.97 to 1.00; panels b-g share a 0-1 scale. Agreement declined across the three tested reuse levels for both rules on all seven metrics. Each point represents one construction and network inference result; no repeated-construction uncertainty intervals are shown. Connecting lines are visual guides between tested constructions. The native network is a computational reference, not experimentally established regulatory ground truth.
