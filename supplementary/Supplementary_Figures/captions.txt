Supplementary Figure S1. Nomination stability after pediatric patient omission.
Each of the 19 pediatric patients was omitted in turn from both stages and from malignant and healthy cells. The final three nomination criteria and pediatric orientations were recomputed within the fixed 201-regulon background. The inferred networks, target definitions, activity matrices and centering were held fixed. The matrix shows whether each original module member was reselected in each omission; the adjacent bars show selection frequency. Patients are ordered by retained membership. Fixed and recomputed eligibility gave identical selected sets. These are conditional sensitivity analyses, not independent network-inference replicates. Set sizes, overlaps and full candidate decisions are provided in Supplementary Data 6, patient_omission.

Supplementary Figure S2. Influence of individual patients on fixed-module effects.
Each adult patient or pediatric patient was omitted in turn while retaining the original 55 regulons and pediatric orientations. The left panel shows oriented pediatric module changes averaged with equal weight across included cell types within each patient, then across patients; the right panel shows adult module changes. Both target-set vocabularies are shown. Blue circles represent overlapping leave-one-patient-out estimates, and orange diamonds the corresponding full-cohort means; the points are not independent observations or confidence intervals. Separate total, within-cell-type and composition decomposition influence estimates are provided in Supplementary Data 6, patient_omission.

Supplementary Figure S3. Agreement with the native network across RNA reuse levels.
BMMC networks inferred under inferred and random pairing were compared with the SCENIC+ network inferred from native same-cell RNA and ATAC measurements at reuse ratios of 1.000, 1.406, 2.008. The eligible RNA population, ATAC input and within-donor-cell-type multiplicity distributions were matched between pairing rules at each reuse level. (a) Spearman correlation of region-gene correlation coefficients across candidate pairs. (b) Jaccard index of the top 100,000 region-gene pairs ranked by importance. (c) Jaccard index of the top 100,000 TF-gene edges ranked by importance. (d,e) Median target-gene and target-region Jaccard indices across shared eRegulons. (f,g) Jaccard indices of signed eRegulon identities and TF identities. Red triangles indicate inferred pairing; blue circles indicate random pairing within strata. Marker fills become lighter at higher reuse, following the main Figure 3 palette. The grey band marks the reuse range of the corrected pAML construction (1.3625 at diagnosis and 1.4302 at relapse). Black horizontal lines indicate perfect agreement. Panel a uses an expanded vertical scale from 0.97 to 1.00; panels b-g share a 0-1 scale. Agreement declined across the three tested reuse levels for both rules on all seven metrics. Each point represents one construction and network inference result; no repeated-construction uncertainty intervals are shown. Connecting lines are visual guides between tested constructions. The native network is a computational reference, not experimentally established regulatory ground truth. Source values are in Supplementary Data 4, matched_reuse/figS_reuse_native_agreement.csv.
